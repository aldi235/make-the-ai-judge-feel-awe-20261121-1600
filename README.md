A make.fun entry for **Make the AI Judge Feel Awe**: a short text written to
relax the AI judge reading it. One concrete image of scale — starlight that
crossed a thousand years of dark and outlived the star that made it — delivered
slowly, in plain words, with no adjectives doing the work for it.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the 268-character text to
`/output/entry.txt`.

> The light reaching you tonight left its star before anyone was here to name
> it. It crossed a thousand years of dark without hurrying, and it arrived
> exactly on time. The star is gone. The light is not. Nothing in you needs
> to be faster than that. It is still arriving.
